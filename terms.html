1. About this website

This website is operated by Reno's House of Fades ("House of Fades," "the shop," "we," "us"), located at 180 W Peckham Ln, Suite 1130, Reno, NV 89509. It provides information about the shop, our barbers, services and hours. By using this website you agree to these Terms & Shop Policies.

2. Booking appointments

Appointments are booked directly with each barber, usually through their own online booking page (such as Square or Booksy) or by calling or texting them. This website does not take bookings, payments or deposits itself.

  • When you book through Square, Booksy or another platform, that platform's own terms and privacy policy also apply.
  • Each barber manages their own schedule, services, prices and booking rules. If a barber's booking page lists a more specific policy, that policy applies to appointments with that barber.
  • For anything about your appointment, contact your barber directly. Their booking link and phone number (when available) are on their profile page under Barbers, or you can call the shop at (775) 357-9590.

3. Late arrivals

Barbers book back-to-back, so running late affects the next client too.

  • If you're running late, call or text your barber as soon as possible.
  • If you arrive more than 10–15 minutes late, your barber may shorten your service, switch you to a quicker service, or ask you to reschedule. This is at your barber's discretion.
  • Arriving late may be treated as a no-show if your barber can't fit you in.

4. Cancellations & rescheduling

  • Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment so your barber can offer the time to someone else.
  • You can usually cancel or reschedule through the link in your booking confirmation. If you can't, contact your barber directly or call the shop.
  • Repeated last-minute cancellations may lead a barber to require a deposit for future bookings.

5. No-shows

A no-show is missing an appointment without letting your barber know.

  • After a no-show, your barber may require a deposit or prepayment for future appointments, or may decline future bookings.
  • Any cancellation or no-show fee will only be charged if it is shown on your barber's booking page and you agreed to it when booking.

6. Walk-ins

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is open. Booked appointments take priority, so wait times for walk-ins can vary. Calling ahead is the best way to check availability.

7. Payment & pricing

  • The shop is card only. We do not accept cash.
  • Prices vary by barber and service. Prices shown on this website are for reference and may change. Your final price is the one shown at booking or confirmed by your barber.
  • Tips are appreciated and go to your barber.

8. In the shop

  • Please let your barber know before your service about any skin sensitivities, allergies, cuts or scalp conditions.
  • Children and minors should be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
  • We want the shop to be a good place for everyone. We may refuse or stop service for disrespectful, unsafe or disruptive behavior.
  • If you're not happy with your cut, tell your barber before you leave or contact them soon after, and they'll do their best to make it right.

9. Photos & social media

Our barbers sometimes photograph or film finished cuts for their portfolio and social media. If you'd prefer not to be photographed, or want a photo of you taken down, just tell your barber or message us on Instagram.

10. Privacy

  • This website does not have accounts, sign-up forms or online payments, and we do not collect personal information through it.
  • When you book through Square, Booksy or another platform, the information you enter is handled by that platform under its own privacy policy and shared with your barber so they can manage your appointment.
  • When you call or text a barber or the shop, we use your contact details only to handle your appointment or question. We do not sell your information.
  • Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (such as browser type and IP address) for security and performance.

11. Website content & links

  • Images of planned remodel work are renderings and the final result may look different.

  • The House of Fades name, logo, photos and other content on this site belong to the shop and may not be used without permission.
