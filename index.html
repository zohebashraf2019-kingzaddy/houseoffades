The Shop

More than a haircut.
it's the House.

Since 2015, Reno's House of Fades has been the spot for clean fades and sharp line-ups in Reno. Co-owned by barbers Anthony Acosta and Juan Garcia, the shop runs on detail, consistency and real barbershop energy.

The game's always on the screens, the conversation's always going, and every barber takes the time to get your cut exactly right. Walk in as a client, leave like family.
